implementation. The authoritative current document is
[`candidate-v3.md`](candidate-v3.md); earlier candidates are kept for
historical context.

## Reading order

1. **For a quick understanding of the design**: read `candidate-v3.md` sections 0–6.
2. **For the full surface and release plan**: continue through `candidate-v3.md` to §14 (delta against v2).
3. **For background on why a decision was made the way it was**: trace from `candidate-v3.md` §11 (tensions) back through `candidate-v2.md` and the Stage 1 / Stage 2 documents.
